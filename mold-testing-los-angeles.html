Trusted Mold Testing Across Los Angeles County

Los Angeles homes face mold from a mix of coastal humidity, aging plumbing, roof and window leaks, and poorly ventilated bathrooms and kitchens. Whether you're in a mid-century home in the valleys, a hillside property, or a condo near the coast, hidden moisture can feed mold long before you see it.

We come to your property, inspect for visible growth and moisture, and collect air and surface samples that go to an accredited independent lab. You get a clear written report telling you what's actually there — nothing more, nothing less.

When to Test for Mold

  • A persistent musty or earthy smell
  • Past water damage, leaks, or flooding
  • Visible staining or discoloration on walls or ceilings
  • Buying or selling a home and needing documentation
  • Verifying that a completed remediation actually worked
