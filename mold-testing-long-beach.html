Why Long Beach homes and buildings get mold

Long Beach is a coastal port city on the southern edge of LA County. Long Beach sits right on the water, so ocean humidity and salt air keep indoor moisture levels high year-round — especially in homes near the shoreline and harbor.

Long Beach is full of older Craftsman bungalows and waterfront condos, where original windows and dated ventilation trap damp air. The most common triggers we see in Long Beach include ocean humidity and salt air near the shoreline, condensation in older bungalows, and waterfront condo units with limited airflow.

What mold testing in Long Beach involves

Mold testing measures what's actually present — we collect air (spore) samples and surface (swab or tape-lift) samples and send them to an accredited independent lab for identification and spore counts. In Long Beach, that usually means comparing the air inside your home to the outdoor baseline and sampling any suspect spots — the pattern that reveals whether ocean humidity and salt air near the shoreline has led to hidden growth.

We test homes, condos, and commercial spaces from Belmont Shore to Alamitos Beach, and everywhere in between across Long Beach.

What’s included in your Long Beach mold testing

  • Visual assessment of moisture-prone areas
  • Indoor air (spore) samples with an outdoor comparison
  • Surface / swab samples of any suspect spots
  • Moisture-meter readings to find damp materials
  • Accredited independent lab analysis
  • A clear written report with the lab data and next steps
  • Local knowledge of Long Beach’s common moisture sources (condensation in older bungalows)
