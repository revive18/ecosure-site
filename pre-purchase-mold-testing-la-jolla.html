Why La Jolla homes and buildings get mold

La Jolla is an upscale seaside community set on coastal bluffs. La Jolla's bluff-top position means salt air and heavy coastal moisture press against homes daily, and older luxury properties here often hide water intrusion behind expensive finishes.

La Jolla's housing skews to older custom homes and oceanfront condos, where high-end remodels can conceal the very leaks that cause mold. The most common triggers we see in La Jolla include salt air and bluff-top humidity, water intrusion hidden behind luxury finishes, and oceanfront condos with sealed, low-airflow rooms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve all of La Jolla?

Yes — we cover La Jolla and its surrounding neighborhoods, including The Village, Bird Rock, La Jolla Shores, and Mount Soledad, plus nearby San Diego communities. If you're not sure we reach your address, just call and ask.

Does a standard home inspection include mold testing?

Usually not. A general home inspection is visual and may note moisture, but it typically doesn't include air or surface samples sent to a lab. Dedicated mold testing is a separate service.
