Why Burbank homes and buildings get mold

Burbank is a San Fernando Valley city known for its studios and tidy post-war neighborhoods. Burbank's hot, dry valley summers drive constant AC use, and the condensation from aging systems is a common hidden source of mold in ducts and around vents.

Burbank's housing is largely post-war single-story homes with original HVAC and ductwork that were never designed for today's cooling demands. The most common triggers we see in Burbank include AC condensation in aging ductwork, water-heater and laundry leaks in post-war homes, and valley heat driving heavy cooling loads.

What mold testing in Burbank involves

Mold testing measures what's actually present — we collect air (spore) samples and surface (swab or tape-lift) samples and send them to an accredited independent lab for identification and spore counts. In Burbank, that usually means comparing the air inside your home to the outdoor baseline and sampling any suspect spots — the pattern that reveals whether AC condensation in aging ductwork has led to hidden growth.

We test homes, condos, and commercial spaces from Magnolia Park to Chandler Park, and everywhere in between across Burbank.

What’s included in your Burbank mold testing

  • Visual assessment of moisture-prone areas
  • Indoor air (spore) samples with an outdoor comparison
  • Surface / swab samples of any suspect spots
  • Moisture-meter readings to find damp materials
  • Accredited independent lab analysis
  • A clear written report with the lab data and next steps
  • Local knowledge of Burbank’s common moisture sources (water-heater and laundry leaks in post-war homes)
