Why Los Angeles homes and buildings get mold

Los Angeles is a sprawling metro that runs from the coast to the inland valleys. LA's mix of coastal marine layer near the water and dry heat in the valleys creates humidity swings that let moisture linger inside walls and HVAC systems.

Much of LA's housing is mid-century construction with aging plumbing and original ductwork — prime conditions for hidden growth after a slow leak. The most common triggers we see in Los Angeles include window-unit AC condensation in older apartments, slab and plumbing leaks in mid-century homes, and poorly ventilated bathrooms and kitchens.

What a Los Angeles mold inspection covers

A mold inspection is a hands-on walk-through: we look for visible growth and water damage, map moisture with meters and thermal imaging, trace the source, and build a targeted sampling plan. Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve all of Los Angeles?

Yes — we cover Los Angeles and its surrounding neighborhoods, including Hollywood, Silver Lake, Venice, Sherman Oaks, Downtown, and West LA, plus nearby Los Angeles communities. If you're not sure we reach your address, just call and ask.

What's the difference between a mold inspection and mold testing?

An inspection is the hands-on assessment — finding moisture and visible growth and locating the source. Testing is the lab analysis of samples. A complete job usually includes both, and we do them together.

What does the inspector actually look for?

Signs of past or active water intrusion: staining, warping, musty odor, elevated moisture readings, and high-risk spots like bathrooms, under sinks, around windows, and in HVAC systems.
