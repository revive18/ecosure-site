Mold Testing Services

Every service below is testing and reporting only. Eco Sure Testing does not perform remediation, so our findings stay independent and unbiased across Los Angeles and San Diego.

Air Quality (Spore) Testing

We collect indoor and outdoor air samples and compare spore counts. A significantly higher indoor count is the clearest signal of a hidden mold problem. Samples are analyzed by an accredited independent lab.

Surface & Swab Sampling

Tape-lift and swab samples taken directly from discolored or suspect areas identify the specific mold species present and confirm whether visible growth is active mold.

Moisture & Leak Detection

Mold needs water. Using moisture meters and thermal imaging, we locate the moisture source behind the growth so the underlying cause can be fixed — not just the visible symptom.

Visual Mold Inspection

A thorough walk-through documents visible growth, water staining, humidity, and high-risk conditions in kitchens, bathrooms, basements, attics, and crawl spaces before any sampling.

Post-Remediation Clearance Testing

After a remediation company finishes cleanup, we provide independent verification testing to confirm the space has returned to normal fungal levels — the unbiased second opinion that protects you.

Real Estate & Pre-Purchase Testing

Buying, selling, or managing a property? We deliver documented, defensible mold reports that buyers, sellers, agents, and property managers can rely on before closing.
