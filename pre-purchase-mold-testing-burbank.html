Why Burbank homes and buildings get mold

Burbank is a San Fernando Valley city known for its studios and tidy post-war neighborhoods. Burbank's hot, dry valley summers drive constant AC use, and the condensation from aging systems is a common hidden source of mold in ducts and around vents.

Burbank's housing is largely post-war single-story homes with original HVAC and ductwork that were never designed for today's cooling demands. The most common triggers we see in Burbank include AC condensation in aging ductwork, water-heater and laundry leaks in post-war homes, and valley heat driving heavy cooling loads.

Does a standard home inspection include mold testing?

Usually not. A general home inspection is visual and may note moisture, but it typically doesn't include air or surface samples sent to a lab. Dedicated mold testing is a separate service.
