Why Burbank homes and buildings get mold

Burbank is a San Fernando Valley city known for its studios and tidy post-war neighborhoods. Burbank's hot, dry valley summers drive constant AC use, and the condensation from aging systems is a common hidden source of mold in ducts and around vents.

Burbank's housing is largely post-war single-story homes with original HVAC and ductwork that were never designed for today's cooling demands. The most common triggers we see in Burbank include AC condensation in aging ductwork, water-heater and laundry leaks in post-war homes, and valley heat driving heavy cooling loads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve all of Burbank?

Yes — we cover Burbank and its surrounding neighborhoods, including Magnolia Park, Rancho District, Media District, and Chandler Park, plus nearby Los Angeles communities. If you're not sure we reach your address, just call and ask.

What's the difference between a mold inspection and mold testing?

An inspection is the hands-on assessment — finding moisture and visible growth and locating the source. Testing is the lab analysis of samples. A complete job usually includes both, and we do them together.

What does the inspector actually look for?

Signs of past or active water intrusion: staining, warping, musty odor, elevated moisture readings, and high-risk spots like bathrooms, under sinks, around windows, and in HVAC systems.
