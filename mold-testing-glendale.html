Why Glendale homes and buildings get mold

Glendale is a dense foothill city between the Verdugo Mountains and the LA basin. Glendale's tucked-in position against the Verdugos means still air and slow drying, so a leak in a condo or hillside home can stay wet long enough for mold to take hold.

Glendale mixes older single-family homes with a large stock of mid-rise condos and apartments, where shared walls and stacked plumbing spread water problems between units. The most common triggers we see in Glendale include shared-wall leaks in condos and apartments, hillside drainage against foundations, and limited airflow in dense multi-unit buildings.

What mold testing in Glendale involves

Mold testing measures what's actually present — we collect air (spore) samples and surface (swab or tape-lift) samples and send them to an accredited independent lab for identification and spore counts. In Glendale, that usually means comparing the air inside your home to the outdoor baseline and sampling any suspect spots — the pattern that reveals whether shared-wall leaks in condos and apartments has led to hidden growth.

We test homes, condos, and commercial spaces from Adams Hill to downtown Glendale, and everywhere in between across Glendale.

What’s included in your Glendale mold testing

  • Visual assessment of moisture-prone areas
  • Indoor air (spore) samples with an outdoor comparison
  • Surface / swab samples of any suspect spots
  • Moisture-meter readings to find damp materials
  • Accredited independent lab analysis
  • A clear written report with the lab data and next steps
  • Local knowledge of Glendale’s common moisture sources (hillside drainage against foundations)
