Professional Mold Testing Services

Certified sampling and lab analysis to tell you exactly what's in your air and on your surfaces.

Air Quality (Spore) Testing

Air samples measure airborne mold spore concentrations indoors versus outdoors — the standard for confirming a hidden mold problem.

Surface & Swab Sampling

Tape-lift and swab samples from suspect areas identify the specific mold species growing on walls, ceilings, and materials.

Moisture & Leak Detection

Moisture meters and thermal scanning find the water source feeding mold growth, so the root cause gets addressed — not just the symptom.

Visual Mold Inspection

A trained inspector walks the property to document visible growth, water staining, and high-risk conditions before sampling.

Post-Remediation Clearance

Independent verification testing after cleanup, confirming the work actually returned your space to normal fungal levels.

Real Estate & Pre-Purchase Testing

Unbiased mold reports for buyers, sellers, and agents who need documentation they can rely on before closing.

Why an Independent Mold Tester Matters

Many "free mold inspections" come from remediation companies — the more mold they find, the more work they sell. That's a built-in conflict of interest.

Eco Sure Testing does one thing: testing and reporting. We never perform remediation, so we have no reason to over-report or under-report. You get an honest, defensible result you can act on.

  • No conflict of interest — we don't sell cleanup.
  • Accredited lab analysis — samples processed by an independent laboratory.
  • Clear written reports — plain-English findings plus the lab data.
  • Local coverage — serving Los Angeles & San Diego and surrounding areas.
