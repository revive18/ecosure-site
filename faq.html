Mold Testing FAQ

Straight answers to the questions we hear most from homeowners, buyers, and property managers across Los Angeles and San Diego.

How much does mold testing cost?

Cost depends on the size of the property and how many samples are needed. Most residential mold tests fall within a predictable range, and we give you a clear quote before any work begins. Contact us for a free, no-obligation estimate.

How long does a mold test take?

The on-site inspection and sampling visit usually takes one to two hours, depending on the property. Samples then go to an accredited independent lab, and your written results are typically ready within a few business days.

What's the difference between air testing and surface testing?

Air (spore) testing measures how many mold spores are in the air, comparing indoor to outdoor levels to detect hidden growth. Surface testing uses swabs or tape lifts on visible or suspect spots to identify the specific mold species. Many inspections use both for a complete picture.

Do you also remove or remediate mold?

No. We perform testing and reporting only. Because we never do remediation, we have no financial reason to over-report mold. If your report shows cleanup is needed, you're free to hire any qualified contractor you choose.

Why should I use an independent mold tester?

When the company testing your property also sells the cleanup, there's a built-in conflict of interest. As an independent tester, Eco Sure Testing has no stake in the outcome — so your results stay unbiased and defensible, which matters for real estate and insurance situations.

Do I need a mold test before buying a home?

If you notice musty odors, past water damage, or visible staining, an independent mold test gives you documented findings to rely on before closing. Buyers, sellers, and agents often request unbiased testing to avoid disputes.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the greater Los Angeles and San Diego metro areas and many surrounding Southern California communities. Contact us to confirm coverage for your specific address.
