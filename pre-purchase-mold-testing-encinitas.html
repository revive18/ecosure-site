Why Encinitas homes and buildings get mold

Encinitas is a laid-back North County beach town. Encinitas gets steady coastal moisture off the water, and its mix of older beach cottages and newer builds means damp air settles into everything from crawl spaces to newer sealed attics.

Encinitas ranges from vintage beach cottages in Leucadia to newer inland homes in Olivenhain, each with its own moisture quirks. The most common triggers we see in Encinitas include coastal moisture in older beach cottages, crawl-space dampness near the bluffs, and condensation in newer sealed construction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve all of Encinitas?

Yes — we cover Encinitas and its surrounding neighborhoods, including Leucadia, Cardiff-by-the-Sea, Olivenhain, and New Encinitas, plus nearby San Diego communities. If you're not sure we reach your address, just call and ask.

Does a standard home inspection include mold testing?

Usually not. A general home inspection is visual and may note moisture, but it typically doesn't include air or surface samples sent to a lab. Dedicated mold testing is a separate service.

How fast can you turn results around for escrow?

We prioritize real-estate timelines — we can usually schedule quickly and return lab results within a few business days so testing doesn't hold up your contingency period.
