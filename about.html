About Eco Sure Testing LLC

We're an independent mold testing company built on a simple principle: the people who test your property shouldn't be the ones who profit from cleaning it up.

Testing Only. No Conflict of Interest.

A lot of mold "inspections" are offered free by remediation contractors. The problem is obvious once you see it — the more mold they report, the more remediation work they sell. That incentive can distort results.

Eco Sure Testing LLC performs mold testing and reporting exclusively. We do not sell or perform remediation, and we never take referral fees tied to how much mold we find. When we hand you a report, it reflects one thing: what the samples and the accredited lab actually show.

What We Value

  • Independence — testing only, so results stay honest.
  • Accuracy — accredited independent lab analysis on every sample.
  • Clarity — reports written so you actually understand them.
  • Responsiveness — fast scheduling across LA and San Diego.
