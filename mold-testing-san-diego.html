Reliable Mold Testing Across San Diego County

San Diego's coastal climate brings ocean humidity, marine-layer moisture, and condensation that can quietly feed mold — especially in bathrooms, kitchens, laundry areas, and rooms with limited ventilation. Older homes and properties with past leaks are especially prone to hidden growth.

We inspect your property, check for moisture, and collect air and surface samples that are analyzed by an accredited independent lab. Your written report lays out exactly what the testing found, in plain English.

When to Test for Mold

  • A lingering musty or damp smell
  • Past leaks, flooding, or water intrusion
  • Visible staining or discoloration
  • A real estate transaction that needs documentation
  • Confirming a remediation job was done right
